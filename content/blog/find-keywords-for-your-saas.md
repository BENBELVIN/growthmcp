Most SaaS founders guess at keywords. They pick a phrase that sounds right, ship a page, and wonder why traffic never shows up.

The fix is not more content. It is better demand signals before you write.

## Start with what you already rank for

If Search Console is connected, your existing queries are the best seed list. Look for:

- Queries with impressions but few clicks (title or intent mismatch)
- Queries where you rank on page 2 (small improvements can move the needle)
- Queries that match a feature you already ship

These are not vanity metrics. They tell you what Google already associates with your site.

## Use Trends to spot rising demand

Google Trends helps you see whether interest is growing or fading before you commit to a new page.

Pair Trends with your top queries:

1. Pick 3 to 5 queries from Search Console
2. Check trend direction over 12 months
3. Prioritise rising topics where you have product fit

## Turn signals into pages

Every keyword opportunity should map to a clear action:

- **Create new page** when demand exists but you have no strong URL
- **Update existing page** when you rank but CTR or position is weak
- **Target this keyword** when one page almost matches but the angle is off

That is the loop growseo is built around: demand in, ranked actions out.

## What to do this week

1. Export your top 20 queries from Search Console
2. Mark which ones already have a dedicated page
3. Pick one rising query and either create or refresh a page this week

One focused page beats ten vague ones every time.
